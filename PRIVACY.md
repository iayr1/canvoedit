# Canvo Edit privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026. Applies to Canvo Edit version 1.0.0 and later, until
this policy is updated._

## Summary

Canvo Edit edits your photos, videos and designs on your device. We don't collect,
upload or sell your content or personal information.

## What stays on your device

- **Photos, videos and audio you open:** the app copies them into its own private
  storage so your projects keep working. They're never uploaded.
- **Your projects:** saved privately on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Voiceover recordings:** the microphone records only while you're recording a
  voiceover. Recordings are saved privately on your device.
- **Error log:** if something goes wrong, the app writes a short technical log on your
  device. It's only sent anywhere if you choose to share it from the About screen.

## What goes online

- **Fonts:** the first time you use a text font, the app downloads it from Google Fonts.
  That request comes from your device, so Google receives your IP address and standard
  browser-style details. Google's privacy policy applies to that request:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Sharing:** when you share an export, it goes to the app you choose.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Photos and videos | To open the media you choose to edit |
| Save to gallery | To save your finished exports |
| Camera | Only when you choose to record a new clip |
| Microphone | Only while recording a clip or a voiceover |
| Internet | To download fonts |

## Children

Canvo Edit isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their
information.
